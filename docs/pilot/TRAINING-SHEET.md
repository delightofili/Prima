# Prima: training session for a clinic (60 minutes)

*Status: a draft written by the builder on 12 November 2026. A clinician must read it before the first session.*

*A clinic is not trained until this session is done and the table at the end is signed.* This is a MUST in `docs/gate/CLINICAL-SAFETY.md`. Words in bold are the words on the Prima screens.

## Rules for the session

- Use *practice patients only*. No real patient is entered in training, and nobody says a real patient's name.
- Teach in **English**. The pilot runs in English until native speakers have checked the translations.
- Everyone who will use Prima comes: the officer in charge, every nurse and CHEW, and anyone who covers a shift.
- The paper register stays. Say it at the start and at the end.

## Bring

- One phone per person, with Chrome, charged, and the date and time on automatic.
- Internet for the first sign in.
- A printed quick card for each person, and the lost or stolen phone page (section 8 of the officer guide).
- The paper register, and a pen.

## The plan

| Minutes | What you do |
|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | *Say what Prima is, and is not.* Read this aloud: "Prima is a patient register and visit record for primary health centres. It works with no internet. Prima is *not* a diagnostic tool. It does not choose a treatment or a dose. It does not replace your judgement. The clinician stays responsible for every clinical decision." Then say: "Your paper register stays for four weeks. Prima is the second record." |
| 5 to 10 | **Practice mode.** Press **Try Prima with practice patients**. Say that the patients are made up and nothing is sent anywhere. Press **Leave practice mode** at the end of the session. |
| 10 to 25 | *Register and find.* Each person registers three practice patients: an adult, a baby with a guardian, and a child whose name is almost the same as the baby's. Press **Register**. *Before you save, read the name and the age aloud.* Show **This patient may already be registered.** and **A similar patient is already registered.** Show **This is a different person**. Then **Find a patient** and **Scan card**. |
| 25 to 35 | *Visit and vaccine.* Open a patient and press **Add visit**. Fill in the vitals, then press **Save visit**. Read each number back before saving. Show **Flags are a guide, not a diagnosis.** Press **Add vaccine** and **Save vaccine**. Show the **Next dose**. |
| 35 to 45 | **No internet.** Everyone turns on airplane mode and registers one more practice patient. The sync badge says **Offline**. Show **waiting to upload**. Turn airplane mode off. Show **Synced**. Show **Sync now**. Say: "Look at the badge at the start and the end of every shift." If it says **need attention**, tell your admin, and show **Retry the ones that need attention**. |
| 45 to 52 | *Lost phone and privacy.* Read the lost or stolen phone page. Show **Lock now**. Say that nobody shares a sign in or a PIN. The admin shows **Account**, then **Team**, then **Remove**. |
| 52 to 57 | *Reminders are a list.* Open **Dashboard**, then **Reminders**. Read **Sending is not switched on yet.** Say: "Nobody has been told by Prima. Do not tell a family they were reminded." |
| 57 to 60 | *Questions, the quiz, and the signing.* |

## The stop rules, said plainly

Say this slowly, and make sure the officer in charge has heard it:

"If a record is saved on the wrong patient, if records are lost, or if a patient's information is seen by someone who should not see it, *stop using Prima at this clinic*. Go back to paper. Tell us the same day. Prima stays paused until we have looked at it together. A pause is not a failure. It is the plan working."

## The quiz (10 questions)

Read each question aloud and let people answer in turn. The answers are for the trainer.

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is Prima not? | A diagnostic tool. It does not choose a treatment or a dose |
| 2 | What do you do before you save a patient? | Read the name and the age aloud |
| 3 | A reminder is in the list. Does that mean the family was told? | No. Nothing is sent yet |
| 4 | When do you look at the sync badge? | At the start and at the end of every shift |
| 5 | The badge says **waiting to upload** for a long time. What do you do? | Tell the admin. The records are safe on the phone, but not in the cloud yet |
| 6 | A phone is lost. What is the first step? | Tell the officer in charge at once. The admin removes the person |
| 7 | Can two nurses share one sign in? | No. Everyone has their own account and PIN |
| 8 | A number has a flag next to it. What does that mean? | It is a guide for you. It is not a diagnosis |
| 9 | What happens to the paper register? | It stays for four weeks. Both are counted every week |
| 10 | Name one thing that stops Prima at the clinic | A wrong patient, lost records, or a privacy incident |

*Pass mark: 8 of 10 for each person.* Anyone below repeats the part they missed with the officer in charge, and answers again.

## After the session

- Write down every question people asked. They are the first feedback (see `FEEDBACK-TOOLS.md`).
- Give each person a quick card. Pin one next to the paper register.
- Do not let anyone enter a real patient until the builder has told the officer in charge, in writing, that the pilot has started.
