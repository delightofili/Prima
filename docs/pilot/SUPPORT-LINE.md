# Prima: the support line for pilot clinics

*Status: a draft written by the builder on 12 November 2026.* Promise only what you can keep. The times below are proposals: change them to what one person can really answer, before any clinic sees them.

## How a clinic reaches you

- WhatsApp: `[FILL IN: the support number, used only for this]`
- Email: hello@primahealth.com.ng

The WhatsApp number is only for the pilot. Do not use it for anything else, so that a message about a patient problem is never lost among others.

## The one rule for every message

*Never send a patient's name, phone number, address or a photo of the screen.* If a clinic does, do not reply with it, and do not copy it anywhere. Say: "Please do not send patient details. Tell me the time, the screen and what you pressed." Then delete the message.

## What the clinic sends

A short message with five things:

1. The clinic name
2. The time it happened
3. The screen (for example, **Add visit**)
4. What you pressed, and what you expected
5. What happened instead, in your own words

## How fast you answer

`[DECIDE: only promise what you can keep]`

| Level | What it is | You answer within |
|---|---|---|
| 1: stop | A record on the wrong patient, records lost, a privacy incident, or nobody can register | 2 hours, between 7:00 and 21:00. *This triggers the stop rule* |
| 2: working badly | A feature broken, but the clinic can work another way | The same day |
| 3: a question or a small thing | A question, a wish, a confusing word | 2 working days |

Outside those hours, the auto reply (below) tells the clinic to use paper and write the problem down.

## What you do for a level 1 message

1. Reply at once: "Thank you. Please use the paper register now. I am looking at it."
2. Start an incident record (`FEEDBACK-TOOLS.md`). Write the time you were told.
3. Pause Prima at that clinic until it has been looked at. Say so in writing to the officer in charge.
4. Tell the clinical safety lead the same day.
5. If patient information may have been seen by someone who should not have seen it, start the breach steps in `docs/gate/NDPA.md`: tell the clinic within 24 hours, so it can meet its own 72 hours.
6. Write down what happened and what was done. Resume only when you and the clinical safety lead agree, and the officer in charge has been told.

## The auto reply

```
Thank you for writing to Prima. This line is answered from 7:00 to 21:00.
If it is urgent, use your paper register now, write down what happened
and the time, and I will reply as soon as I can.
Please do not send patient names, phone numbers or photos of the screen.
```

## Every day of the pilot (10 minutes)

- Read the messages and answer the oldest first.
- Ask each clinic's admin to look at **Devices** (the **Dashboard**) and tell you if any phone says **Behind or waiting** or **Need attention**.
- Write every problem in the issues log, even a small one.

## Every week

- Count the paper register against Prima with the clinic (see `FEEDBACK-TOOLS.md`).
- Read the issues log for patterns. Two clinics with the same problem is a bug.

## Do not

- Do not ask a clinic to share its screen while a patient is on it.
- Do not log in as a clinic's staff to look at the problem. Use the audit log and the Devices tab, with the admin.
- Do not change anything at a clinic's phone while a patient is waiting.
