# Prima quick card

Prima keeps the record. It does not diagnose and it does not decide. You decide.

## Before you save

*Read the name and the age aloud. Check the card.*

## Every patient

1. **Register**: fill in the form and press **Register**. If you see **This patient may already be registered.**, look at that person first.
2. **Find a patient**, or **Scan card**.
3. **Add visit**: check each number, then **Save visit**. **Flags are a guide, not a diagnosis.**
4. **Add vaccine**: check the date, then **Save vaccine**.

## Every shift, at the start and at the end

Look at the sync badge.

- **Offline**: no internet. Keep working.
- **waiting to upload**: still only on this phone.
- **Synced**: safe in the cloud.
- **need attention**: tell your admin.

## Phone lost or stolen

Tell the officer in charge **now**. The admin opens **Account**, then **Team**, and presses **Remove**.

## Keep the paper register for four weeks

## Stop rule

A wrong patient, lost records, or a privacy problem: *stop using Prima*, use paper, and tell us the same day.
