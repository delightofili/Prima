# Prima: guide for the officer in charge (DRAFT)

_Status: a draft written by the builder on 10 November 2026._ A clinician must read it, and someone must follow it on a real phone, before a clinic uses it. Words shown in bold are the words on the Prima screens. Notes marked `[CHECK ON A PHONE: ...]` are things to confirm with the app in your hand.

## 1. What Prima is, and what it is not

Prima is a patient register and visit record for primary health centres. It helps staff register patients, record visits, vital signs and vaccines, find a patient again, see who is due, and report counts to the LGA. It works with no internet.

Prima is _not_ a diagnostic tool. It does not choose a treatment or a dose. It does not replace your judgement. When Prima flags a value, such as a high temperature or a vaccine that is due, that is a prompt for you and never an instruction. The clinician stays responsible for every clinical decision.

Prima also has a **Practice mode**. Press **Try Prima with practice patients** on the first screen. The patients are made up and nothing is sent anywhere. Use it to train your staff. Press **Leave practice mode** when you are done.

## 2. What you need

- A phone or tablet for each person who records patients, with its own screen lock switched on.
- A charger. Keep the phone charged.
- An email address for each person.
- Internet **once**, to sign in. After that, Prima opens and works with no network.
- Your paper register, kept alongside Prima for the first four weeks.

## 3. Start here (the officer in charge, once)

1. Open Prima and press **Create account**. Type **Your name**, **Email** and **Password**. The password needs at least 8 characters.
2. An email arrives with a code. Type the code and press **Confirm email**. If you see nothing, look in your spam folder, or press **Send a new email**. If you forget your password later, press **Forgot your password?**.
3. On **Set up your facility**, press **Create a facility**. Type the **Facility name** and the **Facility code**. The code is 2 to 5 letters and starts every card number. Press **Create facility**. You become the facility admin.
4. A short welcome follows. Choose your language. Read **What Prima is, and what it is not**. Read **Keeping patients' information private** and tick the box. Press **Start using Prima**.
5. Set a PIN. Open **Account**, then **Security**, then **Change PIN**. Choose a 6 digit PIN. The PIN keeps casual snoopers out. It is a lock, not a safe, so keep the phone's own screen lock on too.
6. Add your staff. Open **Account**, then **Team**, then **Create invite code**. Press **Share on WhatsApp**, or **Copy**. Each code works once and expires in 7 days. Your colleague signs up, then presses **Join with a code**, types the **Invite code** and presses **Join facility**.

## 4. Every day

### Register a patient

1. Press **Register**. The screen is called **Register patient**.
2. Type **Full name**. Choose **Sex**. Give the age with **How to give the age**: **Age** or **Birth date**. If you only know the age, Prima saves it as an estimate and asks for the real date later.
3. Fill in **Village or area**, **Pregnant now** if it applies, and **Reason for visit**.
4. **Phone (optional)** and **Parent or guardian (optional)** can be added now or later.
5. Press **Register**, or **Register and add visit**.

_Before you save, read the name and the age aloud to the patient or to the parent._ Check the age against the child's card. A wrong age gives the wrong vaccine schedule.

If Prima says **This patient may already be registered.** or **A similar patient is already registered.**, look at that person first. Do not make a second record for the same child. If it really is someone else, press **This is a different person**.

After saving you can press **Record the visit**, **View profile**, **Print card** or **Register another patient**.

### Find a patient

Press **Find a patient** on **Home**, or open **Patients**. Search by name, phone, NHIS or card number. You can also press **Scan card** and point the camera at the QR code on the patient's card. If the camera does not work, type the card number instead.

### Record a visit

1. Open the patient and press **Add visit**. The screen is called **New visit**.
2. Check the **Visit date**. Fill in **Complaints**.
3. Under **Vitals**, fill in **BP top number (systolic)**, **BP bottom number (diastolic)**, **Temperature** and **Weight**. Read each number back before you save. **Flags are a guide, not a diagnosis.**
4. Under **Assessment**, add **Diagnosis (optional)** and **Treatment / notes (optional)**.
5. Under **Follow-up**, choose **In 3 days**, **In 1 week**, **In 2 weeks** or **In 1 month**, or set **Next visit date (optional)**.
6. Press **Save visit**.

### Record a vaccine

Open the patient and press **Add vaccine**. Choose the **Vaccine**, check the **Date given**, and press **Save vaccine**. Prima shows the **Next dose**. It refuses a date in the future, or before the child was born. Check that the same dose is not recorded twice.

### Print the card

Press **Print card**. It prints at bank card size. The QR code holds only the card number, so anyone who finds the card cannot read the record from it. Ask the family to bring the card to every visit and keep it dry.

### If you make a mistake

Press **Edit** to correct a patient, a visit or a vaccine. To remove one, press **Delete patient**, **Delete visit** or **Delete record**. Prima asks you to confirm, and you can press **Undo** right after.

### Who is due

Open **Dashboard**. **Overview** shows your numbers. **Follow-ups** lists people who are overdue or coming up, on the Pro plan, with one tap to call. **LGA report** gives the monthly report, on the Basic and Pro plans. **Reminders** is a list only: **Sending is not switched on yet.** Do not tell families they were reminded.

## 5. When there is no internet

Everything you record is saved on the phone at once. It uploads by itself when the connection is back.

At the start and end of every shift, look at the sync badge and tap it for **Sync details**.

- **Offline**: no internet. Keep working.
- **waiting to upload**: it will upload soon. If the number stays high for a long time, tell your admin.
- **Synced**: everything on this phone is in the cloud.
- **need attention**: a record could not upload. It is safe on the phone. Press **Retry the ones that need attention**, then tell your admin.
- **Sign in to sync**: sign in again. Your records are safe on the phone.

Press **Sync now** to try by hand.

_Set the date and time on every phone to automatic._ Prima warns you when the clock is wrong. A wrong clock puts wrong dates on visits, and the phone's changes may not upload or reach other phones.

**Low Battery Mode** is in **Account**. In **Automatic** it turns on at 20% battery and off at 30%, or when charging. It stops syncing in the background. Everything you register is still saved. Press **Sync now**, plug in, or switch it **Off**.

### Two phones of the clinic, no internet

Open **Dashboard**, then **Nearby phones**. Both phones must be of this clinic, close together, on the same Wi-Fi. On the first phone press **Show my code**. On the second press **Scan their code**. Follow the steps and keep both phones open until it says **Done**. A phone with internet then uploads everything. Use Chrome on Android. A phone that has never connected needs to connect to the internet once, to get the clinic key.

If the phones cannot reach each other, use **No Wi-Fi? Send a file**: press **Make and send a file**, send it to the other phone, and press **Open a changes file** there.

### Which phones are behind (admin)

Open **Dashboard**, then **Devices**. Each phone shows **Up to date**, **Behind or waiting** or **Need attention**. **Remove from list** only removes it from this list. It deletes nothing and does not lock the phone.

## 6. Every week

- _Make a backup._ Open **Account**, then **Backup and restore**. Press **Back up now**. A window called **Back up this register** opens. Type a password in **Password (8 or more characters)**, type it again in **Repeat the password**, and press **Create backup**. The file is saved in the phone's downloads. Copy it to a second safe place. Keep the password somewhere safe: without it the file cannot be opened.
- _Restore a backup._ In **Backup and restore**, press **Restore**. In the window **Restore from a backup**, choose the **Backup file**, type the **Backup password**, and press **Check the backup**. Read the table. Restoring only adds what is missing and never deletes anything on the phone. Then press **Restore now**.
- _Check Devices_ (admin). No phone should be behind for long.
- _Check Team_ (admin). Press **Remove** next to anyone who has left.
- _Look at the activity._ Open **Account**, then **Audit log and data requests**. It shows who did what, and when. Read it once a month. It is also where patients' requests to see, correct or delete their records are recorded.

## 7. The Free plan holds 200 patients

Prima warns you before you reach the limit. Past it, **new patients are still saved, but only on this phone, until you upgrade.** Make a backup so they are not lost if the phone is lost. Only the facility admin can pay. Open **Account**, then **Plans**.

## 8. Lost or stolen phone (one page, print it)

1. _Tell the officer in charge at once._ Write down the time you found out.
2. The admin opens **Account**, then **Team**, finds the person and presses **Remove**. They lose access to the records, and their phone is locked the next time it goes online. You can invite them again later with a new phone.
3. Change the password of that person's email account if the phone was open.
4. Tell hello@primahealth.com.ng. Prima will help you decide whether the law requires you to report it, and when.
5. The patient records on a phone are protected by the PIN, but they are not encrypted on the phone. That is why the PIN and the phone's own screen lock matter.

## 9. Rules for staff (suggested: each clinic decides its own)

- Never share your sign in or your PIN.
- Keep the screen lock on. Press **Lock now** when you step away. Prima also locks by itself under **Lock automatically after**.
- Open only the records you need. Prima keeps a log of who did what.
- Do not photograph the screen, and do not send a patient's details by WhatsApp.
- Pressing **Sign out** needs no internet. Records stay on the phone, hidden until someone from this facility signs in.

## 10. If something goes wrong

| You see                                                   | Do this                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No email with a code                                      | Look in spam. Press **Send a new email**. Check the address you typed              |
| Wrong PIN, too many times                                 | Press **Forgot the PIN? Sign in with your password**. You need internet            |
| **need attention** on the sync badge                      | Press **Retry the ones that need attention**, then tell your admin                 |
| A new version message                                     | Finish what you are saving, then press **Update now**                              |
| The camera will not start                                 | Allow the camera for Prima in the browser's site settings, or type the card number |
| A patient you just registered is missing on another phone | Wait for it to sync, or use **Nearby phones**                                      |

## 11. In the pilot

Keep your paper register for four weeks. At the start and end of each shift, look at the sync badge. Once a week, make a backup and check **Devices**. Tell us about every mistake you find, especially a record saved on the wrong patient. Write to hello@primahealth.com.ng.
