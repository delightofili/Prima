## The one page to attach

### What Prima is, and what we ask of you

*What it is:* a patient register and visit record for primary health centres. It helps staff register patients, record visits, vital signs and vaccines, find a patient again, see who is due, and report counts to the LGA. It works with no internet.

*What it is not:* a diagnostic tool. It does not choose a treatment or a dose. It does not replace your judgement. The clinician stays responsible for every clinical decision.

*What we ask of you, for four weeks:*

1. Keep your paper register as you do now.
2. Send everyone who will use Prima to the training session.
3. Read the name and the age aloud before saving a patient.
4. Look at the sync badge at the start and at the end of every shift.
5. Count the paper register against Prima every Friday.
6. Tell us about anything that went wrong, even a near miss.
7. Stop using Prima, and tell us the same day, if a record is saved on the wrong patient, if records are lost, or if someone sees what they should not.
8. Check your clinic's own registration with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as a data controller. Prima does not do this for you.

*What you receive:* the Pro plan free for three months, a training session, and a support line.

*What Prima does with your data:* it stores and syncs your clinic's records for you, only on your instructions. It does not sell them or look at them in the ordinary course of work. A written agreement says the rest.

*The language:* the pilot runs in English. The Igbo, Hausa and Yoruba versions are drafts that native speakers have not finished checking.
